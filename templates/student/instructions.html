Evaluation Scheme
  • Each question carries the marks indicated alongside its statement.
  • Negative Marking is ACTIVE: 0.25 marks will be deducted for each incorrect answer. Unanswered questions do not incur penalties.
  • No Negative Marking: Incorrect answers do not incur score deductions.
  • Question order is randomized individually for your session.
Examination Guidelines & Navigation Rules
  1. Countdown Timer: The session timer runs continuously once started on the server. If you refresh or briefly disconnect, the timer continues.
  2. Question Palette: The right-side palette allows you to jump directly to any question. Colors represent status: Green = Answered, Amber = Marked for Review, Grey = Unvisited / Unanswered.
  3. Autosave: Every choice selection and answer clear action is automatically saved to the institutional server immediately.
  4. Time Expiration: When the countdown timer reaches 00:00, your exam will automatically submit and finalize.
  5. Academic Integrity: Navigating away or closing the browser window does not pause the session. Ensure a stable network connection before starting.
